Orientation

What This Is

This is the visual companion to Algol's Hand. Where that document tells any model how the prose should sound, this one tells any model how the page should look. It exists so that nothing you make ever has to climb back up to this quality again — it starts here, every time. You do not need to read code to use it. You point at it and say make it look like the house style, and any competent model can read this document and obey it.

The entire look is controlled by a small block of named values at the top of every file — the tokens. Change one token and the whole document shifts in concert: make the gold deeper, every heading and divider follows; swap the paper warmer, the whole ground warms with it. You will never have to describe the look again from scratch. It is written down now, in two places: here, explained in plain language, and in the companion starter template, which is a blank document already wearing the style, waiting for words.

◆ The promise

Hand this file (or the starter) to any future session, on any model, and say "house style." That is the whole instruction. Everything needed to honor it is contained in these two documents.

The Concept

The Idea

Three commitments hold the whole aesthetic together. Break any one of them and it stops being the house style.

One — warm light, never clinical

The ground is ivory, not white; the ink is warm near-black, not pure black. A faint paper grain and soft prismatic light-leaks bleed in from the corners. The page should feel like an illuminated manuscript held near a candle — lit from within, never the flat glare of a screen. This is luxury and restraint, not maximalist noise. Whitespace does as much work as ink.

Two — gold is the voice

Gold carries the headings, the eyebrows, the dividers, the drop-caps, the quotes. It is the color of everything sacred and load-bearing. Two golds do the work: a deeper antique gold at text weight, and a brighter gold for gradients and gilt. Used against ivory, gold reads as value, warmth, and age all at once.

Three — the prism is the signature

One white light, split into the colors of the Realms — the visual argument of the whole project, that the cosmos is one source read seven ways. The prism appears as thin accents only: the ribbon pinned to the top of every page, the underline beneath each section heading, the diamond dividers, the spectrum legend, the bar in the footer. It is never a large fill. The body stays calm gold-on-ivory; the prism is the one flourish you remember. Restraint is what keeps it from becoming a rainbow.

◆ Rule of accent

A section, card, or vignette may carry one accent hue, set via --accent. Neutral or non-Realm pieces default to gold. Never tint a whole section in a strong color — accents live in borders, dots, small fills, and left-rules, not backgrounds.

◆ Paste this at the top of any file — it is the entire palette
/* THE TOKENS — change these, the whole document changes */
:root{
  /* ground & ink — never pure white, never pure black */
  --paper:#FBF7EE;   --paper-2:#F6F0E2;
  --ink:#2B2317;     --ink-soft:#5A4E3A;  --rule:#E4D8BE;
  /* gold — the voice */
  --gold:#9A7B1F;   --gold-bright:#C9A227;   --gold-pale:#E9DCB0;
  /* the prism — thin accents only */
  --zephyros:#8AA63A;  --pyrthera:#C7572B;  --thalassor:#2E6FA3;
  --ferridane:#A66A2E; --tenebralis:#6A3D9A; --luxenfall:#C9A227;
  --glacia:#5C97B8;
  --prism:linear-gradient(90deg, var(--tenebralis), var(--thalassor),
    var(--glacia), var(--zephyros), var(--luxenfall), var(--ferridane), var(--pyrthera));
  --maxw:760px;
}

The Letters

The Type

Three typefaces, each with one job. They never trade roles. Using anything else — Inter, Roboto, Arial, system fonts, Space Grotesk — breaks the style instantly.

Live · the three voices

FRAUNCES · display, headings, drop-caps, pull-quotes The Texture of Days
SPECTRAL · all body text, the reading voice One candle in every city. Still dark, but still lit — and the light is one, though the colors are many.
MARCELLUS · eyebrows, labels, nav, meta (uppercase, letter-spaced) Moirai Codex · Cultural Architecture

The roles, fixed

Fraunces is the display face — warm, high-contrast, soft, with real personality at large sizes. It carries titles, every heading, the drop-cap that opens a section, and pull-quotes (always italic for quotes). Push its optical-size setting high for big type. Spectral is the body — an elegant reading serif that holds long passages without fatigue; light weight (300) for italic asides, regular for prose. Marcellus is the inscriptional label face — Roman, carved, used only small, uppercase, and widely letter-spaced for eyebrows, navigation, metadata, and tags. The contrast between soft Fraunces and carved Marcellus is part of the signature.

Scale

Every size uses clamp() so type breathes between phone and desktop without breakpoints. Titles clamp(2.7rem, 8vw, 5.1rem); section headings clamp(1.9rem, 5vw, 2.9rem); body holds at 19px (18px on small screens); line-height stays generous at ~1.7 for the reading column.

Behavior

Motion & the Small Print

Motion is subtle and serves the light, never the eye's distraction. Three movements only: the prism gradients drift slowly across the ribbon, underlines, and bars (a 16–22s loop, so it shimmers rather than scrolls); sections fade up gently as they enter view; and the accordion + rotates to × on open. Everything honors prefers-reduced-motion — a reader who asks for stillness gets a fully static, equally complete page.

◆ Always

  • One readable column, ~760px max, generously margined.
  • Mobile-responsive by default, via clamp() — no fragile breakpoints.
  • Vanilla JavaScript only, and only for reveal + nav highlight.
  • The prism kept thin: ribbon, underline, divider, legend, footer bar.

◆ Never

  • localStorage / sessionStorage — they fail in this environment.
  • Large prism fills, neon, or more than one accent per element.
  • Generic fonts or purple-gradient-on-white "AI" looks.
  • A new layout language invented per document.

Operating Instructions

For the Machine

This is the block to hand any model — paste it, or just say "follow the Gold-on-White house style." It is written for an LLM, in the imperative.

◆ House Style — directives for any LLM
PRODUCE a single self-contained .html file in the Moirai Codex
"Gold on White / prismatic" house style. Obey all of the following.

1. FONTS. Import Fraunces, Spectral, Marcellus (one Google line).
   Fraunces = display/headings/drop-caps/pull-quotes (italic for quotes).
   Spectral = ALL body text. Marcellus = eyebrows/labels/nav/meta, small
   + uppercase + letter-spaced. Never Inter, Roboto, Arial, system, or
   Space Grotesk.

2. GROUND. Ivory paper (--paper #FBF7EE), warm ink (--ink #2B2317).
   Never pure white or black. Add faint grain + corner prism light-leaks.

3. GOLD is the voice. Headings, eyebrows, dividers, drop-caps, quotes
   are gold. --gold #9A7B1F for text, --gold-bright #C9A227 for gradients.

4. THE PRISM is the signature — one light split into the Realm hues.
   Use THIN accents only: top ribbon, section-heading underline, diamond
   dividers, spectrum legend, footer bar. NEVER a large fill. Body stays
   calm gold-on-ivory. Restraint is what keeps it from being a rainbow.

5. ACCENT. Each section/card carries ONE hue via --accent (defaults
   to gold). Accents live in borders, dots, small fills, left-rules.

6. COMPONENTS. Compose only from the kit: masthead, sticky nav,
   section heading + .uline, .lead drop-cap, .diadiv divider, .spectrum
   legend, .realm accent card + details.thread accordions, .vignette,
   .pull quote, .feature block, footer coda. Do not invent new ones.

7. LAYOUT. One column, --maxw 760px, generous space, clamp() type
   scale, mobile-responsive. Refined/editorial/illuminated — NOT maximal.

8. MOTION. Subtle only: prism "drift", scroll fade-up, accordion
   +→× rotate. Always honor prefers-reduced-motion. Vanilla JS only.
   NO localStorage / sessionStorage.

9. SOURCE OF TRUTH. Copy the :root tokens and component CSS from the
   starter template VERBATIM. Change content, never the system.

◆ The shortest version

Ivory ground, gold voice, one thin ribbon of broken light. Fraunces, Spectral, Marcellus. Build from the kit. Keep it calm and let the prism be the only thing that shimmers.
