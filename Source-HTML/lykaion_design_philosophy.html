ORIGIN
The Wolf Sang First
When a Lykon pack howls, the sound travels not just through air but through the crystal lattice in the bedrock. The Old Lykaion monks felt the wolves singing through the stone and sang back. Call and response, not domestication. The wolves sang first. The people answered.
◇
PHILOSOPHY
The Self Is the Ore
Transcension is the ontology of the Will — true power is earned internally through the refinement of one's own spirit. The Lykaion treat their own bodies and souls as raw material. The mountain doesn't apologize to the gravel.
△
SACRED ART
The Art of the Forged Self
Put something into your body. Make it part of you. Stone into skin. Galvaneum into bone. The mystical integration of materials into living flesh until the boundary between body and material dissolves. Nothing runs on circuits. A Galvaneum arm has living metal fused to living muscle fused to living nerve, all animated by the same vital energy.

THE UNIFYING PRINCIPLE
The metal is alive because they are alive. Nothing has seams. Nothing was bolted on. The integration is biological in the deepest sense.
The beast isn't a costume. It's a revelation of what was always there, the same way Edgerinne submitted to pressure until the diamond emerged from within the stone.
